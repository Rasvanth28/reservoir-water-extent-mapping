# Link-only prior-art check: foundation models, label-free water change, and consensus labels

**Scope and standard.** This covers 2020–2026 peer-reviewed papers, conference papers, and arXiv preprints. I include only papers whose title, authors, venue/year, and working DOI/arXiv/publisher link I could verify from the available indexed material. I do **not** infer an “outperforms its label source” result unless the linked abstract or paper explicitly states it.

## What was not found

- **No linked paper found** that fine-tunes Prithvi, SatMAE, or Clay specifically on spectral-index-threshold or global-product pseudo-labels and explicitly reports that the fine-tuned model outperforms that same label source.
- **No linked deep-learning paper found** that detects reservoir/lake water-extent change from Landsat time series while training with no manual labels.
- **No linked paper found** that combines all three ingredients—foundation-model fine-tuning, label-free Landsat water-change detection, and multi-product consensus labels—in one system.

## Closest papers to the combined idea

1. **Wang et al. (2021)** — closest to automatic spectral-index/clustering pseudo-labels plus SAR–optical foundation-style fine-tuning; lacks Landsat reservoir change and multi-product consensus. [web:57]
2. **RSMA (2024)** — closest to multi-product consensus labels and an explicit gain over a source product; lacks a geospatial foundation model and Landsat water-change task. [web:59]
3. **HESS hybrid water-extent approach (2020)** — closest to label-free Landsat water-extent gap filling; lacks deep learning and foundation models. [web:47][web:52]
4. **PRE land-cover mapping (2024)** — closest to pseudo-label refinement/expansion for land cover; lacks multi-product consensus and water-change application. [web:84]
